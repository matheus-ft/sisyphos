# Photo credits

`farnese-front.png` is the Farnese Hercules, Museo Archeologico Nazionale,
Naples, cut out of its background with `scripts/statue/cutout.swift` and
resized. The photograph is by Euridici, released into the public domain (CC0):
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Heracles._Napoli_archeological_museum.jpg>.

No back view of the same statue under a free licence, at a usable angle, has
been found yet: the CC0 one by the same photographer
(<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Heracles,_back._Napoli_archeological_museum.jpg>)
is taken from below, with visitors in it.
